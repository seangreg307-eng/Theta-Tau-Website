    • Become A Member of Theta Tau

    Being a member of Theta Tau Fraternity offers distinct advantages over membership in some local organization. Some benefits are: Recognition, Lifetime membership, National and regional meetings, Interaction, Awards, Publications, Leadership training, Volunteer opportunities.

    Being a member of the oldest and largest engineering fraternity is not important in itself—people are. A member’s professional and personal development is the benefit of membership. Life after college is important, and membership in Theta Tau is for life, not just for a few collegiate semesters. Once you have completed your student years, you will have formed lifelong friendships and will continue to receive value as an alumnus.

    How to Join!

    We recruit new members in the beginning of every semester. Be sure to attend our rush events for Spring 2014! Rushing a fraternity is the process of getting to know more about the organization, what they are all about and meeting the brothers. During rush, interested students are introduced to the brothers through various activites hosted throughout the week. After getting to know the brothers for a few weeks, of the select potential new members, will begin the pledge process. This pledge period tests those potential new members dedication to representing the 3 pillars of Service, Professionalism, and Brotherhood. Pledging requires your complete dedication and time commitment. We hope to see you this coming January! Be sure to attend our rush events or email us at ruthetatau@gmail.com for more information!

    If you are interested in joining, sign-up here and fill out the Greek Recruitment Registration Form

    Requirements for Membership

  • Full time engineering student at Rutgers University.
  • Must have completed at least one semester as a full time student at Rutgers University.
  • Complete Greek Recruitment Registration Form
